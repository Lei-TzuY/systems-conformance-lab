# Multipart form-data structural reduction

The multipart/form-data interoperability target has a domain-aware reducer for stable differential failures. The reducer operates on the same fixed, target-owned boundary used by the Python and Node adapters and only accepts the canonical CRLF framing emitted by the conformance corpus.

Reduction is intentionally fail-closed. Inputs with a preamble or epilogue, LF-only framing, malformed or non-ASCII header lines, a missing closing boundary, or ambiguous/non-canonical boundary framing are rejected rather than repaired. This prevents reduction from changing parser policy while searching for a smaller witness.

For canonical inputs the reducer can remove complete parts and shrink part bodies. Header reduction recognizes only exact target-owned filename-star forms. Fully percent-encoded terminal `filename*=UTF-8''...` and deterministic `filename*=UTF-8'en'...` payloads may be shortened only by whole `%HH` octets while charset and language metadata remain byte-for-byte intact; hexadecimal spelling is preserved whether uppercase or lowercase. The mixed-representation mutator has a separate narrow contract: `filename*=UTF-8''%HH...` with exactly one leading percent octet followed only by RFC 5987 attr-char bytes. Its reducer keeps that percent octet and at least one literal attr-char, shrinking only the literal suffix so the witness cannot collapse into a different all-percent or attr-char-only representation. Mixed payloads with a language tag, other charsets, unsafe suffix bytes, trailing parameters, malformed escapes, and other Content-Disposition or Content-Type forms are never normalized or rewritten. Every emitted candidate is deterministic, unique, and strictly smaller in encoded bytes.

The integration tests validate the reducer against the real Python stdlib multipart path and Node `Response.formData()` target. A padded `charset=iso-8859-1` witness is discovered as a product mismatch, reduced while preserving its stable failure signature, written as a repro bundle, and replayed through the normal differential harness. Real-target filename-star validations cover empty-language uppercase and lowercase all-percent escapes, the fixed `en` form, and the mixed percent/attr-char representation. Each path is discovered from a matching canonical seed and reduced without crossing its representation contract while retaining the original failure signature and replayability.
